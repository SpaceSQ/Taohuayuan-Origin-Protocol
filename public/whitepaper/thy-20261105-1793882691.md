Security Architecture of S2-CheckSum (Redacted Public Disclosure)To prevent unauthorized batch spoofing or identity tampering, the underlying mathematical weights and modulo transformations of the S2-CheckSum Engine are classified as Level-5 Confidential and execute exclusively within the closed-source backend of the S2 Rainbow Bridge (S2RB).   Positional Sensitivity & Anti-Transposition:
Every character slot within the 20-character payload ([Pos 1–20]) is bound to a distinct, non-public positional weight factor. Any single-character alteration or adjacent transposition (e.g., swapping AB to BA) deterministically invalidates the terminal 2-letter signature.   Compact Orthogonal Signature Space:
By mapping the weighted payload into a dual uppercase alphabetical space (A-Z), the engine achieves 676 orthogonal verification states within just 2 characters—preserving a compact 22-character footprint without exposing raw cryptographic hashes.   Strict Input Sanitization:
Any identifier failing the exact 22-character length check or containing hyphens, whitespace, or non-[A-Z0-9] symbols is rejected immediately at the edge prior to checksum evaluation.   5.
